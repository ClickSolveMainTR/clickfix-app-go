The email channel turns mail sent to your support address (e.g. `support@yourcompany.com`) into inbox conversations. Your replies reach the customer as email and stay in the same thread.

There are two connection methods:

| Method                           | When                                                                                                        |
| -------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Forwarding**                   | You forward your support address to a routing address unique to you. Recommended.                           |
| **My own mailbox (IMAP + SMTP)** | ClickFix reads your mailbox over IMAP and sends replies over SMTP. Use it when you can't set up forwarding. |

## Connect with forwarding [#connect-with-forwarding]

1. Choose **Channels → Add channel → Email** and set **Connection method** to **Forwarding**.
2. In **Routing address**, enter the first part of the address (3-64 characters: lowercase letters, digits, dots or dashes). Leave it empty to have one generated.
3. Fill in **From address** and **From name**. If the from address is empty, replies go out from the routing address.
4. After **Connect**, the screen shows your routing address.
5. In your mail provider (Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Yandex, etc.) create a rule that forwards mail sent to your support address to that routing address.

The Reply-To address is always the routing address, so customer replies come back to ClickFix even before you set up forwarding.

### Verify your sender domain [#verify-your-sender-domain]

To send replies from your own domain (`@yourcompany.com`), verify it:

1. In the channel settings, enter your domain under **Sender domain** and click **Register**.
2. Add the DNS records listed on screen at your DNS provider.
3. Click **Verify**. Once the status is **Verified**, replies are sent from your domain.

DNS changes can take from a few minutes to a few hours to propagate. If the status is **Pending verification**, try again later.

### Your own SMTP server [#your-own-smtp-server]

Choose **My own SMTP server** as the sending provider to send replies through your SMTP server. You'll need the SMTP host, port, username, password and TLS mode.

## Connect over IMAP and SMTP [#connect-over-imap-and-smtp]

1. Set **Connection method** to **My own mailbox (IMAP + SMTP)**.
2. Under **Incoming mail (IMAP)** enter the IMAP host, port, username, password, TLS mode and the folder to read.
3. Under **Outgoing mail (SMTP)** enter the SMTP details. The **From address** is usually the mailbox's own address.
4. Optional settings:
   - **Initial sync (days)**: how many days of mail to import on first connect (1-365, default 3).
   - **Move after import**: a folder imported messages are moved to.
   - **Mark imported messages as read**.
5. Check the details with **Test connection**, then save.

> For Gmail, Outlook and Yandex use an **app password** instead of your account password.

TLS mode can be **Automatic (by port)**, **SSL/TLS** or **STARTTLS**. Passwords are stored encrypted; leave a password field empty when editing to keep the stored password.

## How it works [#how-it-works]

- Messages are threaded into the right conversation using the `References`, `In-Reply-To` and `Message-ID` headers.
- Quoted history and signatures are stripped so you see the visible reply. CCs and attachments are kept.
- Automatic replies (out-of-office, mailing lists) are ignored to prevent loops.
- Bounced mail marks the related message as failed.
