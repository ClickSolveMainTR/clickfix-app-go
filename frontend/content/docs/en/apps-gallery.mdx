---
title: Apps gallery
description: Add ready-made apps to your ClickFix workspace.
---

Open **Organization → App management → Gallery**, select a card and enter your HTTPS URL. The gallery includes order lookup, Calendly, Uptime Kuma, Google Docs, Metabase and OpenStreetMap.

Gallery apps display your URL without receiving conversation data. For conversation context and panel actions, [build your own app](/docs/apps?lang=en).
