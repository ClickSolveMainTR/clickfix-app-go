This guide walks you from creating an account to answering your first customer conversation in ClickFix. The set-up wizard ticks off each step automatically as you use the product.

## 1. Create an account [#1-create-an-account]

1. Open [getclickfix.com](https://getclickfix.com) and follow the sign-up link.
2. Sign up with your name, surname, email and password, or with an allowed social provider.
3. Verify your email address.

If self sign-up is closed, the page says so; contact us for a demo in that case.

## 2. Set up your organization [#2-set-up-your-organization]

In ClickFix, your company's account is called an **organization**. Channels, conversations, contacts and settings belong to the organization and are never shared with other organizations.

1. Enter your company or brand name in **Organization name**. Your customers see this name in the widget and in emails; you can change it later.
2. Click **Open organization**. Your account becomes the owner of this organization.

### Set up with AI or a ready-made template [#set-up-with-ai-or-a-ready-made-template]

The set-up wizard can prepare your organization for you:

- **Set up with AI**: learns about your business from your website and a few short questions, then suggests teams, tags, SLAs, canned replies, starter articles and the AI agent's persona.
- **Pick a ready template**: start from a template that fits your industry.

Either way, you review and edit every suggestion before it is applied. If your plan does not include AI or you are out of credits, the template closest to your answers is personalized for you instead.

## 3. Invite your team [#3-invite-your-team]

1. Open **Organization → Members** in the left menu.
2. On the **Invitations** tab, send an invitation to your teammate's email address and pick a role.
3. The invitee joins with their own account through the link in the email.

The default roles are **Owner**, **Admin** and **Member**. You can create custom roles under **Organization → Roles**. Manage teams, skills and working hours on the **Teams** page.

## 4. Connect your first channel [#4-connect-your-first-channel]

1. Open **Channels** in the left menu and click **Add channel**.
2. Pick the channel type and follow the steps on screen.

Messages start flowing into the inbox as soon as the channel is connected. See [Channels](/docs/channels?lang=en) for every channel's set-up.

## 5. Add the web widget to your site [#5-add-the-web-widget-to-your-site]

1. Open **Web widget** in the left menu and create a widget.
2. Paste the one-line snippet from the **Install** tab into every page of your site, before the `</body>` tag.
3. Open your site and send a message from the widget to try it.

Details: [Web widget](/docs/web-widget?lang=en).

## 6. Receive your first conversation [#6-receive-your-first-conversation]

Send yourself a test message through the channel you connected. Set-up is complete when the message lands in **Inbox → All conversations**. Continue with [Inbox basics](/docs/inbox?lang=en).

## 7. Mobile app [#7-mobile-app]

With the ClickFix iOS and Android app you get push notifications, reply to conversations and manage your availability. Once you are signed in on the app, you can also sign in on the web without typing a password by choosing **Sign in with QR code** on the sign-in screen.
