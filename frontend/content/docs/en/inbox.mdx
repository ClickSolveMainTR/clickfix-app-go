Messages from every channel arrive in one inbox as conversations. This page covers the basics you need day to day.

## Views [#views]

The **Inbox** section of the left menu has three built-in views:

| View              | Shows                                     |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| All conversations | Every conversation you are allowed to see |
| Assigned to me    | Conversations assigned to you             |
| Unassigned        | Conversations nobody owns yet             |

Inside the inbox you also have **Mentions**, **My teams**, **SLA 1 hour** and **SLA breached** filters. Save a filtered list with **Save this view**, and share it with your team with **Share with the organization** if you like.

## Conversation statuses [#conversation-statuses]

| Status   | Meaning                                                                         |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Open     | Waiting for your team                                                           |
| Pending  | Waiting for the customer                                                        |
| Snoozed  | Hidden until the time you choose (1 hour, 3 hours, tomorrow morning, next week) |
| Resolved | The issue is solved; you can reopen the conversation if needed                  |
| Closed   | The conversation is closed                                                      |

SLA clocks pause while you are waiting for the customer (pending, snoozed).

## Assignment [#assignment]

- Assign a conversation to a teammate or take it yourself.
- With automatic routing on, new conversations are distributed by your rules and agents' capacity. Manage the rules under **Team status → Routing**.
- When a teammate has the same conversation open, the conversation shows who is viewing it, so two people don't write a reply at the same time.

## Replies and internal notes [#replies-and-internal-notes]

- A **reply** goes to the customer through the channel the conversation came from.
- An **internal note** is visible only to your team. Mention a teammate in a note to bring them into the conversation.

## Canned replies and macros [#canned-replies-and-macros]

**Inbox → Canned replies** has two tools:

- **Canned replies**: type `/` in the reply box and pick a shortcut; variables are filled from the conversation. They can be personal or team-wide.
- **Macros**: open with `M` in a conversation; the actions you defined (tag, status, assignment and so on) run in order.

## Merging conversations [#merging-conversations]

If the same customer opened two conversations about the same thing, use **Merge conversation** to move one into the other. The selected conversation's messages, tags, participants and side conversations are moved, and it is closed. This cannot be undone.

## Copilot [#copilot]

While you write, Copilot can suggest a reply, summarize the conversation, rewrite, translate, change the tone, shorten or expand your text, suggest the next step and answer questions about the conversation. Details: [AI](/docs/ai?lang=en).

## Keyboard shortcuts [#keyboard-shortcuts]

Press `?` in the inbox to see every shortcut. Common ones: next/previous conversation, reply, internal note, resolve/reopen, assign, take, tag, snooze, apply macro and the Copilot menu.

## SLA [#sla]

Define response and resolution targets, business hours and holidays under **Organization → SLA and business hours**. Conversations close to or past their target appear in the **SLA 1 hour** and **SLA breached** filters.

## Customer identity and context [#identity-and-context]

- **Verified badge:** a contact who proved their identity in the widget with `user_hash` carries the **Verified** badge; so does the company when its id is signed too. The contact card and the conversation panel distinguish three states: **verified**, **anonymous**, **self-named**. The conversation list can filter by them.
- **External id:** the contact's and the company's id in your own system shows on the card and is searchable; the API and webhooks return it as `external_id` / `company_external_id`.
- **Custom fields:** accepted values (plan, licence expiry…) show on the contact and company cards and in the conversation's right panel; an undefined field shows a warning with one-click definition.
- **Screen context:** which screen the customer wrote from and their latest events in the conversation header; also usable in workflow conditions.
- **Records read from your system:** with [Connect your system](/docs/integration-guide/system-binding?lang=en) set up, the customer's invoice, order and other concepts are read in the right panel and the defined actions run from there.

## Instant reply [#instant-reply]

Every new conversation written during working hours gets an automatic acknowledgement ("received, estimated time") within 10 seconds; outside working hours the single-source offline text. These show as system messages in the conversation and do not stop the SLA first-response timer. Setting: [Automation → Welcome](/docs/automation?lang=en#instant-reply).

## FAQ [#faq]

**What does "self-named" mean?** The contact or their channel provided a name, email or phone, but it was not proven with `user_hash`. To count them as verified add [identity verification](/docs/integration-guide/identity?lang=en) to your site.

**How do I write to a customer before they visit the site?** Create the contact by external id through the API and open a conversation with `contact_external_id`; once the user signs in with `identify` they see it in the widget. From the panel: **New conversation** on the contact card.

**Copilot versus Assistant?** Copilot works on text in the reply editor; [ClickFix Assistant](/docs/ai?lang=en#assistant) sets up and tests the panel.
