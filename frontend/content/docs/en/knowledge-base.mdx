The knowledge base is where you write answers to frequent questions and step-by-step fixes. The same articles are used in three places:

- Your public **help center**,
- Search inside the **web widget**,
- Customer-facing replies from the **AI agent**.

Manage it under **Organization → Knowledge base**.

## Writing articles [#writing-articles]

1. Start with **Articles → New article**.
2. Write the title and body. The editor supports headings, lists, code, quotes, tables, images and links.
3. Under **Article details**, set:
   - **Address (slug)**: shown in the article link (lowercase letters, digits and dashes).
   - **Language**: Turkish or English.
   - **Visibility**: **Public** articles appear in the help center, the widget and customer-facing AI replies. **Internal** articles are visible only to your team and the AI agent.
   - **Collection** and **Tags**.
4. **Save draft** or **Save and publish**.

Drafts are invisible until published. Every save creates a new version. A published article's address can't be changed, so old links and AI sources don't break. Use **Unpublish** to take an article down.

## Collections and categories [#collections-and-categories]

- **Collections** are the main sections of the help center and appear as cards on its home page (e.g. Billing, Account, Integrations).
- **Categories** group articles under a collection.

A collection that still contains articles or categories can't be deleted; move them first.

## Help center [#help-center]

Your public articles appear in your organization's help center:

```text
https://getclickfix.com/help/<organization-address>
```

**Open help center** on the knowledge base page takes you there. The help center has:

- Search, collections, recently updated and related articles.
- A Turkish / English switch (English pages open with `?lang=en`).
- **Was this article helpful?** under each article. You see the votes per article in the knowledge base.
- An automatically generated sitemap for search engines.

## Community forum [#community-forum]

The community forum is a public space where your customers ask questions and answer each other. It lives under your help center:

```text
https://getclickfix.com/help/<organization-address>/community
```

Manage it from **Community forum** in the left menu:

1. Create at least one category on the **Categories** tab. The forum appears in the help center once the first category exists. A category's address can't be changed once published.
2. Every topic and reply from visitors goes to the **Moderation** tab before it's published. Mark it **Approve**, **Reject** or **Spam**.
3. The **Topics** tab lists published topics; you can **Mark as accepted answer** on a reply.

Posts with suspicious instructions or many links are flagged in moderation. **Review with AI** spends AI credits to record a suggestion; it never publishes or rejects the post by itself.

### Forum question from a conversation [#forum-question-from-a-conversation]

You can create a public forum question from a customer conversation. The conversation text isn't copied; only the text you write goes to the moderation queue. Remove personal details (name, email, order number and so on) from it.

## FAQ [#faq]

**Which articles does the AI agent use?** Only **public** and **published** ones. Drafts and internal articles never reach the agent; records read from your system through [Connect your system](/docs/integration-guide/system-binding?lang=en) are added per customer.

**Can I show an article to specific customers only?** Yes; article targeting uses list-type [custom field](/docs/integration-guide/custom-fields?lang=en) options (e.g. plan = Pro).

**How does search work in the widget?** It searches published articles; results follow the visitor's language.
