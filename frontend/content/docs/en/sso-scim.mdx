With single sign-on (SSO) your team signs in to ClickFix through your own identity provider (IdP). With SCIM 2.0, users and groups are created, updated and deactivated automatically from your identity provider.

SSO, SCIM and IP restrictions are **Enterprise plan** features. The settings are under **Organization → Security**.

## How it works [#how-it-works]

- To sign in with SSO, a user must already be **provisioned through SCIM** and active. ClickFix never links users automatically by email address alone.
- The SCIM `externalId` **must equal** the OIDC `sub` or the SAML `NameID`. Use a stable, immutable identifier.
- An SSO session is valid only for that organization. When it expires, users sign in again through the identity provider.

## 1. Configure SSO [#1-configure-sso]

Under **Organization → Security → Single sign-on (SSO)**, choose a **Protocol**.

### OIDC [#oidc]

1. Create an OIDC application (authorization code flow) in your identity provider.
2. Add the **Redirect URI** shown in ClickFix to your IdP application.
3. Enter in ClickFix: **Issuer**, **Client ID**, **Client secret**, **Authorization URL**, **Token URL** and **JWKS URL**. All URLs must use `https://`.
4. Tick **SSO enabled** and save.

ClickFix uses PKCE (S256) and validates the ID token's RS256 signature, issuer, audience and expiry. The client secret is stored encrypted and never shown again; leave the field empty to keep the stored value.

### SAML [#saml]

1. Create a SAML application in your identity provider using the **ACS URL (Assertion Consumer Service)** and **SP Entity ID / metadata URL** shown in ClickFix.
2. Paste your IdP's **metadata XML** into ClickFix or pick it with **Upload XML file** (up to 1 MB).
3. Tick **SSO enabled** and save.

For SAML:

- The IdP must send **signed**, **unencrypted** assertions. Encrypted assertions are not supported.
- Sign-in always starts from ClickFix. IdP-initiated sign-ins are rejected.

## 2. Provision users with SCIM [#2-provision-users-with-scim]

1. After saving the SSO configuration, click **Create / rotate token** under **SCIM provisioning**.
2. Enter the **SCIM base URL** and **SCIM token** shown into your identity provider's SCIM / provisioning settings. The token is shown **only once**.
3. Assign users and groups to the ClickFix application in your IdP.

Creating a new token invalidates the old one immediately; update your IdP when you do.

### Supported SCIM operations [#supported-scim-operations]

| Resource | Operations                                                                                                                         |
| -------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Users    | Paged list, GET, POST, PUT, PATCH, DELETE. `userName` and `externalId` equality filters                                            |
| Groups   | Paged list, GET, POST, PUT, PATCH, DELETE. `displayName` and `externalId` equality filters, adding and removing members with PATCH |

Not supported: Bulk operations, password changes, sorting, ETags and other filter expressions (they return `400`). SCIM requests are limited to 120 per minute per organization.

Notes:

- Deactivating or deleting a user removes their membership in this organization and their team memberships; the ClickFix account itself isn't deleted.
- To prevent lockouts, members with the **owner** or **admin** role can't be removed through SCIM.
- Groups map to teams in ClickFix. A group PUT replaces the member list entirely.

## 3. Test sign-in [#3-test-sign-in]

1. Click **Test sign-in**. You're sent to your identity provider and sign in.
2. If the response is valid and a matching SCIM-provisioned user is found, you see a success message.

If the test fails, check the issuer, client details, metadata and the user's SCIM record.

Share the **Sign-in link for members** with your team; it starts your organization's SSO sign-in directly.

## 4. Enforce SSO [#4-enforce-sso]

With **Require SSO** on, only SSO sessions can access the organization; members who signed in with a password are blocked.

- Enforcement can only be turned on from an **SSO-verified session**. Verify your session with **Test sign-in** first.
- Before turning it on, make sure admins can sign in with SSO. A broken SSO configuration can lock your team out.

## IP restrictions [#ip-restrictions]

Under **IP restrictions** you can limit access to the organization to specific IP addresses or CIDR blocks (e.g. `203.0.113.0/24`). An empty list means no restriction; up to 100 rules are allowed.

The restriction applies to every request, including the API and the mobile app. ClickFix won't let you save a list that doesn't contain your current IP; add it with **Add my IP**.
